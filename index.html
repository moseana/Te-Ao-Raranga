TE AO RARANGA ☰


Hā ki roto
Breathe in

Hā ki waho
Breathe out

Let breath bind your body, mind, and spirit.
Through the past, to futures and back to presence.
Sink into your consciousness and
birth sensitivity to the tides of wairua,
to energy flowing within, and all around.
