TANGATA



BEN GIRVEN

Ngāti Kahungunu ki te Wairoa


Engaging the tuākana, tēina relationship between myself and the whenua, photography is a tool of listening to Ranginui and Papatūānuku's teachings. My MFA project centers around the creation of on-location photograms in Wairoa and Te Whanganui-a-Tara whenua, awa and ngahere as a tool to aid my immersive journey with the maramataka. This practice reciprocally deepens my environmental korero. Kahungunu tātai arorangi (astronomy) guides my practice of environmentally responsive making, while considering the interconnected relationships between ourselves, atua, tīpuna, ngā rangi tūhāhā, (the cosmos/the universe), and te whenua.




TIANA KRAFFT-SOFFE

Te Ātiawa, Ngāti Apa, Ngāti Kuia, Hihifo


Tiana Krafft-Soffe is a multimedia artist of Tongan (Hihifo), Māori (Te Āti Awa, Ngāti Apa, Ngāti Kuia) and German descent based in Te Whanganui-a-tara. Her creative practice weaves together dance/movement, video, animation, and audio to create multi-layered, time-based artworks. Through her artwork she seeks to activate an energetic awareness in the viewer.

SYLVIE AROHA

Ngāti Kahungunu ki te Wairoa


Sylvie is a dance artist living in Te Whanganui-a-tara. She is curious about dance as a process of change, connection, deconstruction, trying, questioning, feeling and being - a way of 'holding on' within dynamic and unstable environments. Sylvie trained at Tarrant Dance Studios, and has danced at workshops in Aotearoa, Salzburg, Vienna, Brussels, West Kerry, London, and Barcelona. She is studying her Masters in Dance Movement Therapy through Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland.
