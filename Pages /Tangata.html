TANGATA



BEN GIRVEN

Ngāti Kahungunu ki te Wairoa


Ben Girven is a contemporary art photographer, engaging the camera-less medium of the photogram. His whakaahua (photographs/photography) take form on location, with a pulse of light, in whenua, ngahere, awa and moana, embodying maramataka (Māori lunar calendar) as methodology. Girven extends perceptions of wā (space-time), light and energy, exploring relational spaces activated in wānanga, through connection with art, each other, and the world. Te taiao (our environment/ourselves) is creator, camera, and darkroom, blurring distinction between the agents of photographic engagement and affirming whakaahua to be an indigenous medium. He received a Bachelor of Design in Mātauranga Toi Māori and Photography, and a Master of Fine Arts at Te Kunenga ki Pūrehuroa, Massey University.




TIANA KRAFFT-SOFFE

Hihifo, Te Ātiawa, Ngāti Apa, Ngāti Kuia, German


Tiana is a multi-disciplinary artist, frequently weaving together movement, lens, sound, and digital practices. She centers movement of the body as a channel for intuitive and genealogical pathways to knowledge. Through movement, she explores how she can connect to ancestry, to tūpuna and atua, and employs visual and sonic art forms to ignite a bodily awareness and spark a somatic experience. Tiana has trained in ballet and contemporary dance, and has completed a Bachelor of Design majoring in Mātauranga Toi Māori and Photography. She is currently working towards her Master of Fine Arts at Te Kunenga ki Pūrehuroa, Massey University.






MADE IN COLLABORATION WITH



SYLVIE AROHA

Ngāti Kahungunu ki te Wairoa


Sylvie is a dance artist living in Te Whanganui-a-tara. She is curious about dance as a process of change, connection, deconstruction, trying, questioning, feeling and being - a way of 'holding on' within dynamic and unstable environments. Sylvie trained at Tarrant Dance Studios, and has danced at workshops in Aotearoa, Salzburg, Vienna, Brussels, West Kerry, London, and Barcelona. She is studying her Masters in Dance Movement Therapy through Waipapa Taumata Rau, University of Auckland.
